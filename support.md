# Lowtrace support

**Contact:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/chethan3040/lowtrace-privacy/issues). We usually reply within 2 working days.

## Turn Lowtrace on

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Go to **Apps > Safari > Extensions**.
3. Turn on every **Lowtrace** blocker (there are six).
4. Open Lowtrace: it should say **All 6 blockers are on**.

## Ads still show on a site

- Check that every Lowtrace blocker is on (see above), then reload the page.
- Check that the site isn't in Lowtrace > **Allowed Sites**.
- Some ads can't be blocked by any Safari blocker. Send us the page address and we'll
  look at it.

## A site looks broken

Allow ads on that site: in Safari, open the **Share** menu and tap **Allow Ads on This
Site** (scroll down the menu if you don't see it). Or add the site in Lowtrace > **Allowed
Sites**. Please tell us which site, so we can fix the filters.

## Ads in YouTube, Instagram, games or other apps

iOS doesn't let any app block ads inside other apps, or in browsers other than Safari.
Lowtrace works in Safari and in apps that open links with Safari's in-app viewer.

## Lowtrace Plus

- **One-time purchase**, no subscription. Shared with your family through Family Sharing.
- **New phone or reinstall:** open Lowtrace > **Lowtrace Plus** and tap **Restore
  Purchases**, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

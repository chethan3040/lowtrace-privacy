# Lowtrace privacy policy (iPhone and iPad)

_Last updated: 2026-10-18_

Lowtrace is an ad blocker for Safari on iPhone and iPad, published by Itathya technologies
LLP ("the developer"). It has no account, no analytics and no server.

## What Lowtrace collects

**Nothing.** Lowtrace does not collect, store, sell or share personal data or browsing
data. The developer receives no data from the app.

## How blocking works

Lowtrace gives Safari lists of blocking rules. Safari applies them itself, inside Safari.
Lowtrace doesn't see the pages you visit, what was blocked, or anything you type.

The one exception is a page you choose to share with Lowtrace's **Allow Ads on This Site**
action in Safari's share menu. The action receives that page's address and keeps only its
site name (for example `example.com`) in your allowlist, on your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Your settings:** your allowlist (the site names where you allowed ads, added in the app
  or with Allow Ads on This Site). It is stored on your device, in storage shared only
  between the Lowtrace app and its own Safari extensions. It is not sent anywhere.
- **The blocking rules** Lowtrace gives Safari.

## Purchase

You buy Lowtrace on the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's
privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Lowtrace and the developer never
see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Network requests

Lowtrace makes no network requests of its own; filter lists ship with app updates. If a
future version downloads updated filter lists, this policy will be updated first and the
download will contain only rule files, with no personal data sent.

## Filter lists

Blocking rules come from EasyList, EasyPrivacy and Fanboy's lists, by The EasyList authors
(https://easylist.to/), used under CC BY-SA 3.0.

## Children

Lowtrace collects no data from anyone, including children.
